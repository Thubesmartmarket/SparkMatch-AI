FAQ

Is this an emergency electrical service?

No. For sparks, burning smells or shock risk, switch off power if safe and call a qualified electrician immediately.

Will an electrician contact me automatically?

No. You decide who receives your brief.

Does this replace a COC inspection?

No. Compliance work is done by the electrician.

Are quotes binding here?

No. Formal quotes come from the electrician.
